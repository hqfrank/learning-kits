A limit calibrated from one legitimate run rejects correct work half the time; the binding ratio then says how close a pass came

The frozen evaluator (note B2) gates each check against a limit calibrated from legitimate runs: correct implementations that are not bit-identical (note B1). Its binding ratio is the largest measured ÷ limit over all checks; below 1 passes, and promotion also needs it at or below the margin cap. Here one check (output distance from the trusted reference) is calibrated as multiple × the largest of K legitimate distances, and two checks have fixed design limits. Move the calibration count, the multiple and the candidate's measurements.

Model choice of this page: legitimate distances are half-normal with unit scale (one page-seeded set of ten is drawn); a broken control sits a fixed offset beyond legitimate noise. The two probabilities are averages over calibration draws, computed by exact integration. The soft-miss limit, the decision-time limit, the multiple and the margin caps are design parameters of the harness. Nothing here is a measurement.
