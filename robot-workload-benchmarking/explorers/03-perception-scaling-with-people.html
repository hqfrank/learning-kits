As people are added, CPU post-processing overtakes the GPU — and a software rate cap hides it

People perception pays a fixed per-frame cost plus a per-person cost on both the GPU (small networks) and the CPU (NMS, tracking, Hungarian matching, projection, filtering). This page predicts per-frame latency on each side, finds which side binds, and the crossover N where CPU overtakes GPU. All defaults are illustrative — the shapes are the lesson, not the numbers.
