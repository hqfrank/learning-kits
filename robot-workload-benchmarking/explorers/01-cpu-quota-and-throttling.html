A CPU quota caps delivered cores against the service's demand — and throttled_usec is not that demand

A container with a CFS bandwidth quota (cpu.max = quota/period) is parked once it spends its quota, until the next accounting period. Move the demand, the quota, and the period to see delivered cores, the fraction of periods throttled, how long a thread waits each period, and the extra latency a request that arrives mid-period pays. The deployed quota is the baseline; an uncapped run is a separate call-out, never the headline.
