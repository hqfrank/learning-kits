A short window cannot prove a low miss rate: the exact Poisson interval, and how many samples a p99 needs

Deadline misses are counted as rare Poisson events. From a count over a known time this page computes the exact two-sided Garwood 95% interval on the rate, in the browser, and calls PASS / UNRESOLVED / FAIL against bands you set. A second panel turns an output rate and a window length into a sample count, and shows why a stable p99 needs several thousand samples.
