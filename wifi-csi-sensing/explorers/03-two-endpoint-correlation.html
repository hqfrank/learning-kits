Motion near the shared AP moves both links together, and motion near one device moves mostly that device's link

One AP and two sensing devices give two links that share the AP end. A single link cannot tell motion near its AP end from motion near its device end (B4). Comparing the two links can. Move the person along the AP → device 1 link and off to the side. The page shows each link's variance, their cross-correlation over a 4 s window, and the decision: no motion, AP side, near device 1, near device 2, or ambiguous.

Phenomenological model, not a propagation simulation. The shared AP-end component is built into the model, as B4 describes it. The page shows how the decision rule behaves, not why the physics produces the shared component. The weights and the wall factor are page assumptions assumption.
