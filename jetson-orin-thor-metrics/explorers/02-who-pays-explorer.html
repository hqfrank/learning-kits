Who pays? Co-tenancy on one Thor GPU is decided by the sharing discipline and the governing mechanism — not by TOPS

Put a victim (a latency-critical control loop) and an aggressor on one GPU and the GPU is a fixed pie: the discipline decides which tenant absorbs the deficit (§B.6, L4). Pick the victim class, the aggressor, the topology and the CPU memory-load, and the explorer names the governing mechanism, predicts the victim's p99 slowdown, and overlays the measured p99 from the book's tables beside the predicted one.
