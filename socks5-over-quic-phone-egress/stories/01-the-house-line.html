Where the house line breaks

  • A call carries voice. Here each call is a TCP byte stream, and the conversation is end-to-end TLS that the helper passes on but cannot read.
  • The trunk is not a circuit. It is UDP packets; a lost packet is sent again, and only its own channel waits.
  • The front desk checks no caller. Anyone can dial the extension; the handshake (voiceprint, language, ticket) is the only gate.
  • A voice can be imitated. A certificate fingerprint cannot be answered without the private key.
  • The helper is software on your phone, and it runs only while the chat is open. The "intercom" also covers the cloud's own metadata address, not only rooms in your house.
