An SM can be full with few warps: blocks per SM are the minimum over four limits

An SM of an Orin-class board (example: Jetson AGX Orin, compute capability 8.7) holds a kernel's blocks up to four limits: warps, blocks, registers and shared memory. The binding fill is the largest used fraction of any of the four on the busiest SM in the first wave; at ≥ 90 % that SM has little room for another kernel's block. A kernel with more blocks than the SMs can hold at once runs in waves. Set a launch configuration and see which resource binds (note B2). Occupancy and registers are taught in nvidia-gpu-anatomy, note 03.
