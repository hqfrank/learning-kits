Beats add up to a dwell time, the reset turns it into a cycle, and the cycle sets visitors per hour: if more people arrive than the cycle serves, the queue grows all day

Write the visit as timed beats (note A2). Change any beat, the reset, the queue-friction factor and the arrival rate, and watch the cycle, the visitors served per hour, and the queue over a show day. The arithmetic runs both ways: the page also says how much the cycle must shrink to keep up. Every default is an illustrative value from ../constants.json; the queue is a deterministic page model with steady arrivals. Nothing here is a measurement.
