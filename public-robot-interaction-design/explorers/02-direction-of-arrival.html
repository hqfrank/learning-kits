A microphone pair a few centimetres apart turns a talker's bearing into a delay of a few hundred microseconds: coarse enough that the bearing should gate faces, not choose between them

Sound reaches the nearer microphone first. The time difference of arrival (TDOA) between microphones x apart is x·sin θ / c. Invert it and you have the bearing θ. Move the talker, the spacing, the number of microphones and the timing noise to see the estimate and its error. Then place one or two faces to see the fusion rule of note B2: the bearing binds to a face inside its window, abstains when two faces share the window, and flags a voice with no face. The physics is public arithmetic from ../constants.json. Noise, faces and cases are page inputs, not measurements.
