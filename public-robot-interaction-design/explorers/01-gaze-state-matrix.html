A gaze policy that follows every label flickers; one that holds the addressee and gates each re-aim looks at fewer wrong people, less often

Top-down view of a public demo stand: the robot at the bottom, one to four visitors in front of it. Pick who speaks and whether the robot answers. Each tick, a cell of the gaze state matrix (reasoner state × who the label names) says what the head wants; the gated policy then checks the gates of note B3 (self-speech suppression, slew hold, deadband, dwell, contested) before it re-aims. Compare it with the first design (look at the nearest visitor) and with a policy that follows every speaker label. Every head move is an eased glide with a peak-speed cap. Scene, script and scoring are a page model; the timings are illustrative values from ../constants.json. Nothing here is a measurement.
